"Make a website with AI for free" is one of the most common searches about AI websites, and it is a fair thing to want: you should be able to see whether an idea works before you pay for anything. The catch is that "free" means different things on every tool. This guide lists what you actually get.

I run equipe, one of the tools below, so I have a stake. The facts about other tools come from their own pricing pages, read on 28 November 2026; prices change, so check before you decide.

<p class="answer"><strong>The short answer.</strong> You can make a website with AI for free on most AI builders, or paste a page you made in ChatGPT or Claude into equipe and put it live free. Free plans usually mean a shared address, the builder's branding or a usage limit. For a business, expect to pay about $15 to $50 a month once you want your own domain and no limits.</p>

## What free plans include (read 28 November 2026)

| Tool | What the free plan gives you | The main limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wix | Free to start, no card; AI site builder | Your site lives at a wixsite.com address with Wix branding |
| Lovable | 5 credits a day, up to 30 a month | Published sites open only for people you invite |
| Bolt | 1M tokens a month (300K a day), hosting at a bolt.host address | Bolt branding; a free site goes offline at its monthly limit until it resets |
| Base44 | 25 message credits and 100 integration credits a month, up to 5 apps, with sign-in and a database | A custom domain needs a paid plan |
| equipe | 500 credits when you create your team, 1,000 more when you add a card (no charge); your first version is on us | Free teams stop when the free credit is used; free teams nobody opens for 14 days are deleted after a warning email |

Each of these is a reasonable way to try an idea. Which one is best depends on what you need after the first day.

## Three free ways to get a site up today

**1. Start from a description.** Tell an AI builder or an AI team what you want: "a one-page site for my bakery with the menu, opening hours and a contact form." On equipe the PM asks a couple of short questions, and the median first version across 11 real projects went live in about 40 minutes (as of 27 November 2026).

**2. Bring what you already made in a chat.** If ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini already made you a page, you do not need to start again. Paste the code at [publish what you made with AI](/publish-html/) and it runs in a preview before you even sign up; then it goes live at its own equipe.page address. From Claude specifically, use [From Claude](/from-claude/).

**3. Build a single-purpose page with a free tool.** If what you need is a booking page, the free [booking page maker](/tools/booking-page/) shows a working preview as you type, with no AI and no sign-up. Pair it with the free [privacy policy generator](/tools/privacy-policy-generator/).

## What free usually leaves out

- **Your own domain.** A shared address works for testing, but customers trust yourbusiness.com more. A domain usually costs $10 to $20 a year on top of any plan. Our [domain guide](/blog/buy-and-connect-a-domain/) explains it.
- **No branding.** Most free plans show the builder's name.
- **A contact form that actually reaches you, and a privacy policy.** Check where a form sends its messages.
- **Keeping it running.** Usage limits can take a site offline or stop the building mid-change.
- **Changes after launch.** The first version is the easy part. Ask what each change costs later.

## When paying is worth it

Pay when a real person besides you will use the site: a customer, a student, a client. At that point a broken form or an offline page costs you more than the plan. On equipe, $19 a month keeps a product live with its domain and storage, and you add credits only when you want changes; the [pricing page](/pricing/) has the details.
